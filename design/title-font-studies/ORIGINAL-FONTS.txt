Grain title alphabets 01–20

Original font outlines created for this Grain project in 2026. These title
alphabets were constructed from original polygons and paths. No third-party
font outlines, commercial font files or traced logos were used.

Each font contains G, R, A, I and N, lowercase character mappings to the same
option-specific forms, space, period and hyphen. These are limited title
alphabets, not full type families. Options 10 and 20 use lowercase forms for
both uppercase and lowercase input. Body type remains IBM Plex Mono.

TrueType and WOFF2 versions contain the same outlines. The generator and font
metadata are included alongside the comparison page for future editing.

IBM Plex Mono is a separate body font, distributed under its included SIL
Open Font License. It is not the source for any of these title outlines.

Options 11–20 use original irregular particle contours, clustered grains,
porous ink and fluid stroke paths. Particle placements are deterministic.
Round 1 remains available at round-1.html.
