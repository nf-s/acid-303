Grain title cuts 21–30

These are modified title subsets derived from professional typefaces, not wholly original type designs. All source font software and these derivatives remain under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Original copyright notices and complete licenses are included in licenses/. Reserved Font Names are not used as the modified families’ primary names.

Modifications: static weight selection where applicable; title alphabet subset; normalized 740-unit cap height; modest width adjustments; custom spacing; new Grain Title XX family names. Professional source outlines and curve construction are retained. Supported characters are GRAINgrain, space, period and hyphen. Lowercase maps to the uppercase title forms.
